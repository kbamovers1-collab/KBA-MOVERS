About Our Ankeny Moving Services

Ankeny has earned its reputation as one of the fastest-growing cities in Iowa, and KBA Movers has been right there helping new residents move in. Located just north of Des Moines along the I-35 corridor, Ankeny offers the perfect blend of suburban living and easy access to the metro. Our team makes the trip from our West Des Moines base to Ankeny regularly, and we are deeply familiar with the city's neighborhoods, new developments, and unique moving logistics. From brand-new construction homes to established neighborhoods, we provide professional moving services tailored to Ankeny's needs.

Ankeny Neighborhoods and Areas We Serve

Prairie Trail is Ankeny's signature mixed-use development, featuring modern homes, townhouses, apartments, and retail spaces all within a walkable community. We handle moves into Prairie Trail frequently, navigating its unique layout and coordinating with property managers for multi-unit buildings. Uptown Ankeny offers a charming downtown feel with older homes and local businesses along SW 3rd Street. The new development areas on Ankeny's north and east sides are where much of the city's growth is happening, with entire subdivisions of new-construction homes being built each year. We also serve residents along the Delaware Avenue corridor, near DMACC's Ankeny campus, and throughout the established neighborhoods south of First Street.

New Construction Moves in Ankeny

With so many new homes being built in Ankeny each year, we have developed deep expertise in new-construction moves. We understand the importance of protecting brand-new flooring, freshly painted walls, and pristine fixtures during move-in day. Our team uses floor runners, corner guards, and door frame protectors to ensure your new home stays in perfect condition from the moment you move in. We also coordinate timing with builders and closing schedules so your move happens seamlessly.

Why People Are Moving to Ankeny

Ankeny attracts families with its top-rated Ankeny Community School District, abundant parks and trails, and a strong sense of community. The city's location along I-35 provides easy commutes to Des Moines, and DMACC's main campus brings students and educators to the area. With new restaurants, shopping centers, and recreational facilities opening regularly, Ankeny continues to draw new residents from across Iowa and beyond. KBA Movers is proud to help these families start their Ankeny chapter.
