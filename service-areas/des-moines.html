Des Moines Neighborhoods We Serve

Des Moines is a city of diverse neighborhoods, and we serve them all. Downtown Des Moines and the East Village are booming with loft apartments, condos, and mixed-use developments that require careful navigation of elevators, loading docks, and parking logistics. The Drake neighborhood surrounding Drake University features a mix of student housing, charming Craftsman homes, and rental properties. Beaverdale is one of Des Moines' most beloved neighborhoods, known for its stone-front houses and strong community spirit. The South Side offers affordable housing and a rich cultural heritage with a growing number of young families moving in. We also regularly serve Ingersoll, Waterbury, Sherman Hill, River Bend, and the growing Gray's Station area.

A City on the Rise

Des Moines has experienced a remarkable renaissance over the past decade. The downtown core has been transformed with new residential towers, the Western Gateway Park, and a thriving food and arts scene. Major employers including Principal Financial Group, EMC Insurance, and Meredith Corporation keep the job market strong, drawing new residents from across the country. This growth means a steady demand for professional moving services, and KBA Movers is here to meet that demand with reliable, affordable service.
