Ames Neighborhoods We Serve

From student apartments near campus to family homes in established neighborhoods, KBA Movers handles relocations across all of Ames and the surrounding Story County area.

Campustown

Campustown is the bustling neighborhood adjacent to Iowa State University, filled with apartment complexes, student housing, and local businesses. We specialize in navigating the tight streets and busy parking areas that come with Campustown moves — especially during the hectic August and May turnover periods. Our crew works efficiently to get you in or out on time, even when every other tenant is moving the same day.

Somerset

The Somerset neighborhood on the south side of Ames offers a mix of newer single-family homes and townhomes popular with families and young professionals. KBA Movers provides full-service residential moves in Somerset, including packing, furniture disassembly, and careful transport of all your belongings.

North Ames

North Ames features established neighborhoods with mature trees, larger lots, and a quieter residential feel. Whether you're moving into a home near Ada Hayden Heritage Park or relocating from a property off of Stange Road, our team delivers dependable service with attention to detail.

West Ames & Research Park Area

With Iowa State's Research Park and growing commercial development on the west side, we also handle office and commercial moves in this part of town. From lab equipment to cubicle furniture, we have the tools and experience for the job.

College Student Moving in Ames

Ames sees some of Iowa's heaviest moving activity during university move-in and move-out seasons. KBA Movers offers affordable college student moving packages designed for the unique needs of Iowa State students:

  • August move-in: We help students get settled into dorms, apartments, and Greek housing before the semester starts.
  • May move-out: End-of-year moves, storage transitions, and relocations back home or to summer housing.
  • Mid-year moves: Lease changes, roommate switches, and transfers between housing throughout the year.
  • Budget-friendly options: Labor-only help, small-load moves, and flexible scheduling to keep costs low.

Book early for August and May — these time slots fill up fast.
