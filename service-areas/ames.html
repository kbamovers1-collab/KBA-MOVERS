From Campustown apartments to family homes — professional movers serving the Iowa State University community and all of Ames.

About Our Ames Moving Services

Ames is a vibrant college town approximately 30 miles north of Des Moines, and KBA Movers is proud to extend our professional moving services to this dynamic community. Home to Iowa State University and its 30,000-plus students, Ames has a unique moving rhythm driven by the academic calendar. We understand the seasonal peaks, the tight timelines, and the specific challenges of moving in a college town. But Ames is much more than a university — it is a thriving city with growing residential neighborhoods, a strong tech sector anchored by the ISU Research Park, and families who love calling it home. Whatever your reason for moving in Ames, KBA Movers delivers reliable, professional service.

Ames Neighborhoods and Areas We Serve

Campustown, the bustling area immediately south of the Iowa State campus, is packed with apartments, rental houses, and student-oriented businesses. We handle hundreds of Campustown moves, navigating narrow streets, limited parking, and multi-story walk-ups with efficiency. Somerset on Ames' west side is a popular residential neighborhood with newer homes, townhouses, and a strong family community centered around Somerset Elementary. North Ames features a mix of established and newer residential areas along the North Grand Mall corridor. The ISU Research Park area on the south side of town is home to tech companies and professional offices, and we assist with commercial relocations in this growing hub. We also serve Old Town Ames, the neighborhoods near Ada Hayden Heritage Park, and the developments along South Duff Avenue.

Student Moving Season in Ames

Every August and May, Ames experiences a massive wave of moves as students arrive for the fall semester and depart after spring finals. This creates a high-demand period where professional movers are essential. KBA Movers ramps up our Ames availability during these peak seasons, offering affordable student moving packages designed for apartments and rental houses. We understand that students are often on tight budgets and even tighter timelines, so we offer efficient, no-frills options that get the job done quickly. Whether you are a freshman moving into your first off-campus apartment or a graduating senior heading to a new city, we are here to help.
