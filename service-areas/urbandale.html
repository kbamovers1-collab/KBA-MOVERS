Further west, the Living History Farms area near Hickman Road and the interstate features newer residential developments, including subdivisions with modern homes and family-friendly amenities. We also serve the neighborhoods along Douglas Avenue, the Walnut Creek area, and the growing developments near 156th Street on Urbandale's western edge.
