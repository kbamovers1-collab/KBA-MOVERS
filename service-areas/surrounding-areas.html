Communities We Serve

Beyond our core service areas of West Des Moines, Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, and Ames, KBA Movers covers a wide range of Central Iowa cities and towns. Here are the communities we serve regularly.

Waukee

One of Iowa's fastest-growing cities, Waukee is home to new housing developments, growing families, and a thriving commercial district. We handle residential and commercial moves throughout Waukee, from the established neighborhoods near downtown to the newest subdivisions on the west side.

Clive

Nestled between West Des Moines and Urbandale, Clive offers a prime suburban location along the Greenbelt Trail. KBA Movers provides full-service moving for Clive residents, whether you're relocating within the city or moving to a new part of the metro.

Johnston

Johnston's family-friendly neighborhoods and excellent school district make it a popular destination for homebuyers. Our team is experienced with moves in Johnston's newer developments as well as its established residential areas along Merle Hay Road and NW Beaver Drive.

Grimes

Grimes continues to see rapid growth with new construction and expanding commercial areas. KBA Movers helps families and businesses settle into this booming community north of the metro with reliable, on-time service.

Norwalk

South of Des Moines, Norwalk offers a small-town atmosphere with easy metro access. We serve Norwalk with the same full range of moving services available in our core areas, from packing to specialty item transport.

Pleasant Hill

Located on the east side of the metro, Pleasant Hill is a growing community with a mix of residential neighborhoods and rural properties. KBA Movers handles moves of all sizes in Pleasant Hill, including homes with larger lots and longer driveways.

Altoona

Home to Adventureland and the Prairie Meadows area, Altoona sits on the eastern edge of the Des Moines metro. We provide residential and commercial moving services throughout Altoona and the surrounding area.

Bondurant

Bondurant is one of the metro's fastest-growing smaller communities, with new subdivisions going up every year. Our team regularly serves Bondurant residents who are moving into new construction or relocating within the area.

Extended Service Area

For communities beyond the 30-mile local radius, KBA Movers offers long-distance moving services. We regularly serve these Central Iowa cities and beyond:

Indianola

About 20 miles south of Des Moines, Indianola is home to Simpson College and the National Balloon Classic. We provide both local and long-distance moving services for Indianola residents, students, and businesses.

Newton

Located roughly 30 miles east of Des Moines along I-80, Newton is known for the Iowa Speedway and its historic downtown. KBA Movers serves Newton with residential and commercial moves, connecting this community to the rest of Central Iowa.

Marshalltown

Approximately 55 miles northeast of Des Moines, Marshalltown moves fall under our long-distance service. We provide full-service relocations for Marshalltown residents and businesses looking to move to or from the Des Moines metro area.

Fort Dodge

About 90 miles northwest of Des Moines, Fort Dodge is served through our long-distance moving options. Whether you're relocating to the metro or moving north, KBA Movers can coordinate your Fort Dodge move from start to finish.
