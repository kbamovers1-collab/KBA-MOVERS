Des Moines Metro Suburbs We Serve

The Des Moines metro area is home to a ring of thriving suburban communities, and we serve every one of them. Waukee is one of Iowa's fastest-growing cities, with new subdivisions and a booming population drawn to its excellent schools and family-friendly atmosphere. Clive sits at the heart of the western suburbs, offering a convenient location and strong community identity along the Greenbelt Trail. Johnston, north of Des Moines, combines suburban living with easy highway access and top-rated schools in the Johnston Community School District. Grimes, adjacent to Johnston, is experiencing rapid residential growth with new developments and young families moving in. On the south side of the metro, Norwalk offers a more rural feel with growing neighborhoods and affordable housing. Pleasant Hill and Altoona on the east side provide convenient access to Adventureland and the eastern I-80 corridor. Bondurant, northeast of Des Moines, has seen explosive growth in recent years with new schools and subdivisions transforming this once-small town.

Communities Beyond the Metro

Our service area extends beyond the immediate Des Moines suburbs. Indianola, about 20 miles south of Des Moines, is home to Simpson College and the National Balloon Classic. Newton, approximately 30 miles east along I-80, is known for its historic connection to Maytag and the Iowa Speedway. Marshalltown, about 55 miles northeast, is a regional hub in central Iowa. Fort Dodge, roughly 90 miles northwest, is one of Iowa's larger cities and within our long-distance service range. For these more distant communities, we offer competitive long-distance rates and the same professional care we bring to every local move.
