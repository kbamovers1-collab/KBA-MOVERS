Residential Moving Services in West Des Moines & Central Iowa

From studio apartments to multi-story homes, we handle every detail so you can focus on settling in.

What Is Residential Moving?

Residential moving is the process of relocating all of your household belongings from one home to another. KBA Movers specializes in moves of every size — whether you are leaving a one-bedroom apartment, upgrading to a larger condo, or transferring an entire multi-story house full of furniture and memories. We tailor the crew size, truck capacity, and timeline to match your specific home so nothing is overlooked.

Apartments and Condos

Tight hallways, elevators, and narrow stairwells are no problem. Our crews are experienced with apartment and condo complexes throughout West Des Moines, Des Moines, Ankeny, and Urbandale. We coordinate elevator reservations, follow building move-in and move-out rules, and protect shared spaces during transport.

Single-Family and Multi-Story Homes

Larger homes come with heavier furniture, more fragile items, and additional logistics. We plan the loading order so that the truck is balanced for safe transport, and we stage rooms methodically to get you unpacked faster on the other end.

Furniture Disassembly and Reassembly

Bed frames, sectional sofas, dining tables, and shelving units often need to come apart to fit through doorways safely. Our movers carry the right tools and hardware bags to disassemble items at your old home and reassemble them at your new one, so everything is ready to use when we leave.

What's Included

  • Furniture disassembly at your old home and reassembly at your new home
  • Appliance moving — washers, dryers, refrigerators, and more
  • Wardrobe boxes provided for hanging clothing (no need to remove items from hangers)
  • Floor protection using runners and padding at both locations
  • Door-frame and banister guards to prevent scuffs and dents
  • Moving blankets and stretch wrap for all furniture
  • Placement of furniture and boxes in the rooms you designate

Why Choose KBA Movers for Residential Moving?

  • Right-Sized Crews: We match the number of movers and the truck size to your home so you never pay for capacity you do not need.
  • Property Protection: Floor runners, door-frame guards, and furniture blankets are standard on every residential move — not an up-charge.
  • Appliance Expertise: Heavy appliances require proper dollies and securing techniques. Our crew knows how to disconnect, transport, and position appliances safely.
  • One-Day Completion: Most residential moves within the Des Moines metro are completed in a single day, minimizing disruption to your routine.
