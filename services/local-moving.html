What Is Local Moving?

Local moving covers any relocation within roughly a 30-mile radius of your starting point. Whether you are moving across West Des Moines, from Urbandale to Ankeny, or anywhere else inside the Des Moines metro, KBA Movers delivers a streamlined, stress-free experience. Our local moves are charged at competitive hourly rates with a simple 2-hour minimum, so you only pay for the time you need.

Speed and Efficiency

Because we know the Des Moines metro inside and out, our crews plan the fastest routes and avoid common traffic bottlenecks. Most local apartment and small-home moves are completed in a single morning or afternoon, getting you settled into your new space the same day.

Transparent Hourly Pricing

There are no hidden fees with KBA Movers. You receive a clear hourly rate based on the size of crew and truck you need, with a straightforward 2-hour minimum. The clock starts when we begin loading and stops when the last box is in place at your new home.

Careful Handling from Start to Finish

Even on a quick local move, your belongings deserve full protection. Every piece of furniture is wrapped in moving blankets, and we use floor runners and door-frame protectors to keep both properties damage-free.
