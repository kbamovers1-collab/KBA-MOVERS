Event Setup & Logistics in West Des Moines & Central Iowa

From trade shows to weddings, KBA Movers provides the heavy lifting, setup, and teardown labor so your event runs on schedule.

What Is Event Setup & Logistics?

Running a successful event means coordinating dozens of moving parts — literally. Tables, chairs, staging, displays, audio equipment, and decorations all need to arrive on time, get placed correctly, and be removed when the event wraps up. KBA Movers provides dedicated labor crews for event heavy lifting and logistics across the Des Moines metro area. We work with event planners, venue managers, businesses, and individuals to handle the physical side of event production so you can focus on your guests and programming.

Trade Show Setup and Breakdown

Trade shows demand precision and tight timelines. Our crews unload booth materials, assemble display structures, position signage, and arrange product displays according to your floor plan. After the show, we break everything down, pack it securely, and load it for transport back to your office or storage facility. We work within venue load-in and load-out windows and coordinate with event management teams to stay on schedule.

Venue Setup and Teardown

Whether it is a corporate gala, a fundraiser, a community festival, or a private celebration, venue setup requires reliable labor. Our team handles table and chair arrangement, stage positioning, dance floor assembly, tent setup assistance, and general layout configuration. After the event, we reverse the process — clearing the space, stacking furniture, and loading rental items for pickup or return.

Table, Chair, and Stage Setup

Setting up hundreds of chairs in precise rows or arranging banquet tables with specific spacing takes time and manpower. KBA Movers crews follow your seating chart or layout diagram and get everything positioned efficiently. We also handle portable stage sections, risers, podiums, and AV equipment stands, ensuring stable and level placement for safe use during your event.

What We Handle

  • Unloading and positioning tables, chairs, and staging equipment
  • Trade show booth assembly, display arrangement, and signage placement
  • Dance floor installation and leveling
  • Heavy equipment transport — speakers, lighting rigs, and AV gear
  • Post-event teardown, furniture stacking, and load-out
  • Coordination with venue staff, rental companies, and event planners
  • Same-day setup and teardown for single-day events

Events We Support

Corporate Events and Conferences

Board meetings, product launches, company retreats, and annual conferences all benefit from professional setup labor. We ensure your space is arranged exactly to specification and cleared promptly when the event concludes.

Weddings and Private Celebrations

Your wedding day or milestone celebration should not start with you hauling tables. Our crew sets up reception areas, ceremony seating, photo backdrops, and gift stations so everything is ready before your guests arrive.

Community and Outdoor Events

Festivals, charity runs, farmers markets, and outdoor gatherings require rapid setup across large areas. We bring the manpower to get tents, tables, barriers, and signage in place on time, rain or shine.

Why Choose KBA Movers for Event Logistics?

  • Reliable Crews: Our team shows up on time and works efficiently under tight event schedules. We understand that a late setup is not an option.
  • Heavy Lifting Expertise: Moving staging, AV equipment, and large furniture is what we do every day. We have the equipment and training to handle it safely.
  • Flexible Scheduling: Early morning load-ins, late-night teardowns, weekend events — we work around your event timeline, not the other way around.
