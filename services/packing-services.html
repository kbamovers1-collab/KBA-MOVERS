Professional Packing Services in West Des Moines & Central Iowa

Let our trained packers handle every box, wrap, and label — so nothing arrives damaged and nothing gets lost.

What Are Professional Packing Services?

Packing is often the most time-consuming and stressful part of any move. KBA Movers offers full-service and partial packing performed by trained professionals who know how to protect fragile items, maximize box space, and label everything for fast unpacking. We bring all the materials — boxes, tape, bubble wrap, packing paper, and specialty containers — so you do not have to make multiple trips to the store or wonder if you bought enough supplies.

Full-Service Packing

With full-service packing, our crew handles your entire home from top to bottom. We arrive the day before or the morning of your move and systematically pack every room, labeling each box with its contents and destination room. This option is ideal for busy families, large homes, or anyone who wants a completely hands-off experience.

Partial Packing

If you prefer to pack most of your belongings yourself but need help with fragile or difficult items, partial packing is the answer. Our packers focus on the kitchen, china cabinets, artwork, mirrors, and other breakable items while you handle the straightforward boxes at your own pace.

Material Packages

Not sure how many boxes and supplies you need? We offer three pre-built material packages sized for different homes. The Small package ($75) covers studio and one-bedroom apartments. The Medium package ($125) is designed for two- to three-bedroom homes. The Large package ($150) handles four-bedroom homes and larger. Each package includes boxes in assorted sizes, packing tape, bubble wrap, packing paper, and marker labels.

What's Included

  • All packing materials — boxes, tape, bubble wrap, and packing paper
  • Specialty china and dish-pack boxes with cell dividers for fragile dishware
  • Furniture wrapping with moving blankets and stretch wrap
  • Clear labeling on every box with contents and destination room
  • Mirror and picture-frame boxes for artwork and glass
  • Wardrobe boxes for hanging clothing
  • Unpacking service available — we remove items from boxes and place them where you direct

Why Choose KBA Movers for Packing Services?

  • Trained Packers: Our crew members are trained in professional packing techniques for china, glassware, electronics, and artwork. Fragile items are individually wrapped and cushioned inside reinforced boxes.
  • All Materials Provided: You do not need to buy a single roll of tape. We bring everything required and only charge for what we use, or you can purchase a flat-rate material package in advance.
  • Affordable Packages: Material packages start at just $75 for a small apartment, making professional-grade supplies accessible for any budget.
  • Faster Move Day: When packing is done by professionals in advance, the loading crew can work more efficiently on move day, which often reduces total labor hours and saves you money.
