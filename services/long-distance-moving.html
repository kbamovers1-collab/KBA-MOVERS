Long-Distance Moving Services from West Des Moines, IA

Fully licensed interstate and cross-country relocations — one dedicated truck, one trusted team, door to door.

What Is Long-Distance Moving?

A long-distance move is any relocation that crosses state lines or travels more than 100 miles from your origin. KBA Movers is a federally registered household goods carrier (USDOT #4420459 | MC #1737835), which means we meet every safety, insurance, and compliance standard required by the U.S. Department of Transportation. From Iowa to any state in the country, your belongings travel on a dedicated truck with a crew that stays with your shipment from pickup to delivery.

Dedicated Truck — No Shared Loads

Your possessions are never combined with another household's shipment. A dedicated truck means faster transit times, fewer handling points, and a dramatically lower risk of damage or loss compared to consolidated freight services.

Weight-Based Pricing You Can Verify

Long-distance moves are quoted by weight and distance. Before your move, we provide a binding or not-to-exceed estimate so you know exactly what to expect. On moving day, the truck is weighed before and after loading, and you receive copies of both weight tickets for full transparency.

Storage-in-Transit When You Need It

If your new home is not ready on arrival day, we offer secure storage-in-transit so your belongings stay safe in a climate-monitored facility until you are ready for delivery. There is no need to arrange a separate storage provider.

What's Included

  • Dedicated moving truck for your shipment only
  • Full packing option — we supply all boxes, tape, and materials
  • Weight-based pricing with certified weight tickets
  • Storage-in-transit available at a secure, climate-monitored facility

  • Compliance with all FMCSA regulations (USDOT #4420459 | MC #1737835)

Why Choose KBA Movers for Long-Distance Moving?

  • Federally Licensed and Insured: Our USDOT and MC numbers mean we have passed federal safety audits and carry the insurance required for interstate household goods transport.
  • No Brokered Loads: We are the carrier, not a broker. The crew that loads your home is the same crew that delivers it. You deal with one company from start to finish.
  • Flexible Insurance Options: Choose between basic released-value coverage (included) and full-value replacement protection for complete peace of mind.
  • Accurate Estimates: We perform a thorough inventory assessment so your quote reflects the real weight and scope of your move — no surprise charges at delivery.
