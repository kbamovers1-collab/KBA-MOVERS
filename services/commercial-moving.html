Commercial Moving Services in West Des Moines & Central Iowa

Office relocations, retail moves, and warehouse transfers — executed on schedule with minimal downtime for your business.

What Is Commercial Moving?

Commercial moving covers the relocation of businesses, offices, retail stores, and industrial facilities. Unlike residential moves, commercial relocations require careful coordination around business operations, employee schedules, and sensitive equipment. KBA Movers works with business owners and office managers throughout the Des Moines metro to plan and execute moves that keep downtime to an absolute minimum — whether you are relocating a small office suite or an entire warehouse operation.

Office Relocations

Moving an office involves far more than transporting desks and chairs. Workstations need to be disassembled and reassembled, file cabinets must stay organized, and conference room furniture has to fit the new floor plan. Our crew labels every item by department and workstation number so that employees can return to a fully set-up workspace the next business day.

Retail and Warehouse Transfers

Retail stores and warehouses carry inventory that must be tracked, protected, and relocated without disrupting sales or fulfillment operations. We coordinate with your team to stage the move in phases if needed, ensuring that product flow continues even while the relocation is underway.

After-Hours and Weekend Scheduling

Most businesses cannot afford to close during regular hours for a move. That is why KBA Movers offers evening, overnight, and weekend scheduling. We perform the heavy lifting while your doors are closed so you can open for business at your new location with no gap in service to your customers.

What's Included

  • Office furniture disassembly, transport, and reassembly at your new location
  • IT equipment handling — monitors, desktops, servers, and printers moved with anti-static protection
  • After-hours, evening, and weekend availability to minimize business disruption
  • Labeling and inventory tracking by department or workstation
  • Floor and wall protection at both the origin and destination
  • Coordination with building management for elevator access, loading docks, and parking
  • Secure handling of filing cabinets, safes, and confidential documents

Why Choose KBA Movers for Commercial Moving?

  • Minimal Downtime: We build a detailed move plan with phased timelines so your business loses as few operating hours as possible. Many office moves are completed in a single overnight session.
  • IT Equipment Expertise: Computers, servers, and networking hardware require careful handling. Our crew uses anti-static wrapping and padded crates to protect sensitive electronics during transport.
  • Flexible Scheduling: Evenings, overnights, and weekends are all available. We work around your business hours, not the other way around.
  • Single Point of Contact: Your dedicated move coordinator manages every detail — building access, elevator reservations, parking permits, and crew scheduling — so you can focus on running your business.
