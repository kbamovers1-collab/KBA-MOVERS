Emergency Moving Services in West Des Moines & Central Iowa

When life does not wait, neither do we. Same-day and next-day moving crews dispatched throughout the Des Moines metro.

What Is Emergency Moving?

Emergency moving is a rapid-response relocation service for situations that cannot wait for a standard scheduling window. Whether you are facing an unexpected eviction, a sudden lease termination, a family emergency, or property damage that forces you out on short notice, KBA Movers dispatches a crew to get you moved safely and quickly. We offer both same-day and next-day availability across West Des Moines, Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, Ames, and the greater Central Iowa area.

Same-Day Moves

Need to be out today? Our same-day service puts a crew at your door as quickly as scheduling allows. A same-day scheduling premium of 20% is applied to the standard hourly rate to cover the accelerated dispatch and crew coordination required for immediate response.

Next-Day Moves

If you have until tomorrow, our next-day option gives us slightly more lead time to assemble the right crew and truck for your situation. A next-day scheduling premium of 10% is applied to the standard hourly rate, keeping costs lower while still delivering the urgency you need.

Flexible Hours for Urgent Situations

Emergencies do not follow a 9-to-5 schedule. KBA Movers offers extended and flexible hours for emergency moves, working early mornings, evenings, and weekends to accommodate tight deadlines imposed by landlords, courts, or unforeseen circumstances.
