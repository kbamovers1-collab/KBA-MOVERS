Junk Cleanout Services in West Des Moines & Central Iowa

Full-property cleanouts for homes, garages, basements, estates, and commercial spaces — hauled away and left broom-clean.

What Is a Junk Cleanout?

A junk cleanout is a complete removal of unwanted items from a property. Whether you are clearing out a deceased relative's estate, preparing a rental for new tenants, or finally tackling that overflowing garage, KBA Movers handles the heavy lifting, hauling, and disposal so you do not have to. We serve homeowners, landlords, property managers, and businesses throughout West Des Moines, Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, and the surrounding Central Iowa area.

Residential Cleanouts

From single-room purges to whole-house estate cleanouts, our crew sorts, loads, and removes furniture, appliances, clothing, and general household debris. We work room by room, leaving each space swept and ready for its next chapter — whether that is a sale, renovation, or new occupant.

Garage, Basement, and Attic Clearing

Storage areas accumulate decades of forgotten items. We carefully navigate tight staircases, low ceilings, and narrow doorways to remove everything from old paint cans and broken tools to bulky exercise equipment and outdated electronics.

Commercial and Property Management Cleanouts

Landlords and business owners trust KBA Movers to clear vacated offices, retail spaces, and rental units on tight turnaround schedules. We coordinate around your timeline so the property is tenant-ready or sale-ready as fast as possible.

Why Choose KBA Movers for Junk Cleanouts?

  • One-Stop Solution: We handle sorting, hauling, donation coordination, and disposal in a single visit, saving you multiple trips to the dump and donation center.
