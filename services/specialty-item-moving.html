Specialty Item Moving in West Des Moines & Central Iowa

Pianos, antiques, safes, and heavy equipment — moved with the tools, techniques, and care these items demand.

What Is Specialty Item Moving?

Some belongings are too heavy, too fragile, or too valuable to be handled like ordinary household goods. Specialty item moving is a dedicated service for possessions that require extra equipment, trained handling, and customized protection. KBA Movers has the experience and gear to relocate pianos, antique furniture, gun safes, marble tabletops, sculptures, and industrial equipment safely across West Des Moines, the greater Des Moines metro, and beyond. We assess each item individually and build a handling plan based on its weight, dimensions, fragility, and value.

Pianos and Musical Instruments

Pianos are among the most challenging items to move. An upright piano weighs between 300 and 600 pounds, while a grand piano can exceed 1,000 pounds. Our crew uses piano boards, locking straps, and heavy-duty dollies designed specifically for piano transport. The keyboard lid is secured, pedals are protected, and the entire instrument is wrapped in padded blankets before it leaves your home. We handle uprights, baby grands, and full grands.

Antiques and High-Value Items

Antique furniture, artwork, and collectibles often cannot be replaced at any price. We use custom crating, acid-free packing materials, and soft blanket wrapping to shield these items from vibration, moisture, and impact during transport. For especially valuable pieces, we can arrange additional insurance coverage before move day.

Safes and Heavy Equipment

Gun safes, commercial safes, and heavy workshop equipment present unique challenges because of their extreme weight and awkward dimensions. We categorize items by weight — 300 to 450 pounds, 450 to 600 pounds, and 600-plus pounds — and assign the right crew size, dollies, and ramp equipment for each category. Stairways, tight corners, and elevated placements are all accounted for in advance.

What's Included

  • Custom crating built to the exact dimensions of your item when standard packing is not sufficient
  • Piano boards, locking straps, and keyboard lid protection for all piano moves
  • Multi-layer padding and moving blankets for antiques, marble, and glass
  • Heavy-duty dollies and ramp systems rated for items up to 1,000+ pounds
  • Climate considerations — temperature and humidity monitoring for sensitive instruments and artwork
  • Pre-move assessment to plan pathways, measure doorways, and identify potential obstacles
  • Crew sizing based on weight category: 300–450 lbs, 450–600 lbs, or 600+ lbs

Why Choose KBA Movers for Specialty Item Moving?

  • Item-Specific Equipment: We do not improvise. Piano boards, custom crates, heavy-rated dollies, and stair-climbing equipment are part of our standard toolkit for specialty moves.
  • Weight-Category Pricing: Pricing is based on transparent weight categories (300–450 lbs, 450–600 lbs, 600+ lbs), so you know what to expect before we arrive. No surprises on move day.
  • Pre-Move Site Assessment: Before moving any specialty item, we visit your home or business to measure doorways, assess stairways, and plan the safest route from the item's current location to the truck and into your new space.
  • Insurance Options: Standard liability coverage is included, and we offer supplemental coverage for high-value items so you have full peace of mind throughout the move.
