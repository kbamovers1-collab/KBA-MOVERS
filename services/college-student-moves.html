College Student Moving Services in West Des Moines & Central Iowa

Affordable dorm and apartment moves for students at Drake, DMACC, Grand View, Iowa State, and other Central Iowa schools.

What Is College Student Moving?

College student moving is a budget-friendly moving service tailored to the unique needs of students. Dorm rooms are small, leases turn over on tight schedules, and most students do not own enough furniture to justify a full-size moving truck. KBA Movers offers small-load pricing and dorm-friendly scheduling to help students at Drake University, Des Moines Area Community College (DMACC), Grand View University, Iowa State University in Ames, and other Central Iowa schools move in, move out, and relocate between semesters without breaking the bank.

Small-Load Pricing That Fits a Student Budget

You should not have to pay for a full moving truck when your entire life fits in a dorm room. Our small-load pricing is designed for studio apartments, single dorm rooms, and shared living spaces, giving you professional moving help at a rate that respects a student budget.

Dorm-Friendly Scheduling

Move-in and move-out weekends at colleges are chaotic. Elevators are packed, parking is limited, and time slots are short. Our crew is experienced with campus logistics and works within the check-in and check-out windows set by residence halls, getting you moved without violating housing policies or holding up other students.

Apartment-to-Apartment Moves Between Semesters

Many students switch apartments each year as roommates change and leases rotate. KBA Movers handles the transition between off-campus apartments quickly and affordably, whether you are moving across the street or across town from Drake's neighborhood to the DMACC Ankeny campus area.

What's Included

  • Small-load pricing for dorm rooms, studios, and small apartments
  • Dorm-friendly scheduling around campus move-in and move-out dates
  • Apartment-to-apartment moves within the Des Moines metro and Ames
  • Storage coordination for summer breaks and study-abroad semesters
  • Professional crew with furniture blankets and protective equipment
  • Careful navigation of elevators, narrow hallways, and campus walkways
  • Service to and from Drake, DMACC, Grand View, Iowa State, and other area schools

Why Choose KBA Movers for College Student Moves?

  • Affordable Rates: Our small-load pricing means you only pay for what you actually need to move. No oversized truck fees for a dorm room's worth of belongings.
  • Campus Experience: We know the parking restrictions, elevator reservations, and move-in day procedures at local colleges. Our crew works within those rules so you avoid fines or scheduling conflicts.
  • Storage Coordination: Heading home for the summer or studying abroad? We can coordinate with local storage facilities to get your belongings safely stored until you return.
  • Parent-Approved Reliability: Parents booking a move for their student can count on KBA Movers to show up on time, treat belongings with care, and communicate clearly throughout the process.
