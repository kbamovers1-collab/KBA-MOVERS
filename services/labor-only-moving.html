Labor-Only Moving Help in West Des Moines & Central Iowa

Professional muscle for loading, unloading, and heavy lifting — you supply the truck, we supply the crew.

What Is Labor-Only Moving Help?

Labor-only moving help provides you with a trained moving crew without the truck. If you have already rented a U-Haul, Penske, or Budget truck — or you own a pickup, trailer, or cargo van — KBA Movers sends experienced movers to handle the physically demanding work. We load, unload, rearrange, and lift so you do not have to recruit friends or risk injury. This service is available throughout West Des Moines, Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, Ames, and the surrounding Central Iowa communities.

Loading and Unloading Rental Trucks

Rental trucks save money, but loading them properly requires skill. Our crew knows how to stack boxes, secure furniture, and maximize every cubic foot of truck space. When you arrive at your new location, we unload everything into the correct rooms so you can start unpacking right away.

Furniture Rearrangement and In-Home Moves

Not every move involves a new address. Sometimes you need a heavy sectional couch moved to the basement, a bedroom set swapped between floors, or a home office relocated to a different room. Our labor-only service covers in-home rearrangements of any size, saving your back and your walls.

Heavy Lifting for Deliveries and Purchases

Bought a new appliance, treadmill, or pool table? Our crew handles the heavy lifting for large item deliveries, carrying items up stairs, through tight hallways, and into final position without damaging your home or the item.

  • Flexible Scheduling: Whether you need a crew for two hours or a full day, we match the team size and schedule to your project. Book us for a quick couch move or an all-day truck load.
  • Works with Any Vehicle: Our crew loads U-Hauls, Penske trucks, Budget rentals, personal pickups, cargo vans, and trailers. Whatever you are driving, we make it work.
