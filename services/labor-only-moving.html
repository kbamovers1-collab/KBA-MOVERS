What Is Labor-Only Moving Help?

Labor-only moving help provides you with a professional crew of trained movers without the moving truck. This service is ideal for people who have already rented a truck from U-Haul, Penske, or Budget, borrowed a friend's pickup, or simply need help rearranging heavy furniture inside their home. KBA Movers sends skilled laborers who know how to lift safely, protect your belongings, and work efficiently so you save money on the truck while still getting expert help with the hardest part of any move: the heavy lifting.

Loading and Unloading Rental Trucks

Renting your own truck is a great way to save money on a move, but loading and unloading is where most people struggle. Our crew arrives at your location ready to load your rental truck quickly and strategically, maximizing space and securing items to prevent shifting during transport. When you arrive at your destination, we can meet you there to unload everything and place furniture exactly where you want it. We work with all major rental truck brands throughout the Des Moines metro area.

Furniture Rearrangement and In-Home Moves

Sometimes you do not need to move to a new address. You just need that sectional sofa moved to the basement, a piano shifted to another room, or an entire home office relocated upstairs. Our labor-only service covers in-home furniture rearrangement, saving you from the risk of injury or property damage that comes with trying to wrestle heavy items through doorways and down staircases on your own.

Heavy Lifting for Any Project

Beyond traditional moves, our labor-only crews help with any project that requires strong, careful hands. Need a hot tub moved onto a deck? A pool table relocated to a finished basement? Exercise equipment carried up three flights of stairs? KBA Movers has the equipment and experience to handle heavy, awkward, and oversized items safely in homes and businesses across West Des Moines, Urbandale, Ankeny, and beyond.
